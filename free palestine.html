Why We Stand

The Palestinian people have endured decades of occupation, displacement, and human rights violations. This platform exists to amplify their voices, educate the world, and mobilize support for a free and just future.

To advocate for Palestinian rights through education, awareness, and peaceful action. We work to:

  • Document and share the historical and ongoing struggles of Palestinians
  • Challenge misinformation and biased narratives in mainstream media
  • Support humanitarian efforts that provide aid to affected communities
  • Promote cultural exchange and understanding

We envision a future where:

  • Palestinians enjoy full sovereignty and self-determination
  • All refugees have the right to return to their homes
  • Human rights and dignity are respected for all people in the region
  • Peace is built on justice rather than oppression

Our work is guided by these core principles:

  • Justice: Pursuing equitable solutions that address historical wrongs
  • Solidarity: Standing with Palestinians and all oppressed peoples
  • Truth: Committing to factual accuracy and historical context
  • Nonviolence: Advocating through peaceful means and dialogue
  • Inclusivity: Welcoming all who support human rights and dignity

Historical Context

From the Nakba in 1948 to the present day, Palestinians have faced systemic oppression and loss of land. Understanding the roots of the conflict is essential to advocating for peace and justice.

1917

Balfour Declaration: British support for a Jewish homeland in Palestine without consultation with the indigenous population.

1948

The Nakba — over 700,000 Palestinians displaced from their homes during the establishment of Israel.

1967

Occupation of Gaza and the West Bank begins following the Six-Day War.

1987

First Intifada: Palestinian uprising against Israeli occupation.

1993

Oslo Accords: Interim peace agreement that established the Palestinian Authority.

2000

Second Intifada: Widespread uprising following the failure of peace negotiations.

2005

Israel disengages from Gaza but maintains control of borders, airspace, and waters.

2007

Gaza blockade begins after Hamas takes control of the territory.

Today

Ongoing blockade, settlement expansion, and humanitarian crisis affecting millions of Palestinians.
